About & contact

A student society, run by students, open to everyone.

ISOC DMU has one job: make sure nobody at De Montfort feels like they're doing university on their own. Muslim or not, practising or nowhere near it, you're welcome at almost everything we run.

Everyone is welcome

You don't need to be a member, know anybody, dress a certain way or know a single word of Arabic to walk into one of our events. Most people arrive on their own the first time.

If you're not Muslim and you're curious, come to a discussion night or just ask through the Q&A. Nobody will try to convert you and nobody will be annoyed by the question.
