Report a concern

Harassment, something said or done at an event, a safeguarding worry, or something that just did not sit right.

This form is completely anonymous. It does not ask for your name or email and it does not record who you are. Say as much or as little as you want. You do not need evidence, and you do not need to be sure it counts.

If you are in danger right now

Do not use this form. Call for help.

Police: 999

You never have to report through the committee. These are independent of us and of your course.

Q&A

No question is too small, too awkward, or too 'you'll judge me'.

Ask anonymously and we will come back with an honest, well checked answer rather than a guess. The form does not record who you are, so answers are shared in the WhatsApp group chats and published here.

Ask anonymously

Our question form asks for nothing that identifies you. No name, no email, no login. Ask the thing you have been carrying around.

Genuinely anonymous. We cannot see who sent a question, so nobody on the committee will know it came from you. Answers get published below with no reference to who asked.

· {{ qp }}
Ask your question → Or message @dmuisoc →

An Instagram message is not anonymous, since we can see your handle. Use the form if that matters to you.

Answers go out on WhatsApp

Once the team has researched your question, the answer is shared in the group chats before it appears here. Join one so you actually see the reply, since we have no way of contacting you back.

Who answers these?

Questions go to the educational team first. We research using reliable sources, and anything sensitive or complex is reviewed with the DMU Chaplaincy Imam before it goes up.

A quick disclaimer

Answers here are for general understanding and are checked against reliable sources. For personal rulings or complicated situations we will always point you to a qualified scholar.

Donate

Two things worth giving to.

One keeps the prayer room running. The other keeps the society running. They are separate pots with separate people looking after them, so pick whichever matters more to you, or give a little to both.

Option one

The Prayer Room

The Ramadan iftars, plus the mats, Qurans and upkeep of the space itself. This goes through the university's official donation form, so it is card or Apple Pay and you get a receipt straight away.

· {{ g }}
Donate to the prayer room →

Official DMU donation form, hosted by the university.

Option two

ISOC DMU

The events, the trips and hikes, the snacks at every circle and everything else we put on through the year. This one is handled by us, so speak to the treasurer for your side and they will sort you out with the details.

· {{ g }}

How to give

Let the sisters' treasurer or the brothers' treasurer know and they will give you the instructions. Message the Instagram and say which one you need, or catch either of them at any event.

Message the treasurer →

Small and regular beats big and rare.

Nobody is expecting a fortune from a student. Three pounds covers the tea at a halaqah. If forty people gave the price of one coffee a month, the next trip would be paid for before anyone was asked to chip in.

{{ t.amount }}

{{ t.covers }}

“The most beloved deeds to Allah are those done consistently, even if small.” Whatever you can spare, given quietly and often, is the kind that lasts.

Where ISOC money goes

{{ sp.label }}{{ sp.pct }}

Every penny is held in the society account at the Students' Union and the accounts are read out at the AGM. Prayer room donations are separate and handled by the university.

Sadaqah, zakat or a one off

Tell the treasurer if your gift is zakat and we will keep it separate, since zakat can only be spent on those eligible to receive it.

Give something other than money

Dates, water, prayer mats and Qurans are always needed, especially in Ramadan. Drop them at the prayer room or message us first.

Message @dmuisoc →

Sponsor an iftar

Families, alumni and local businesses sponsor a full evening in Ramadan. Speak to the treasurer, or email the chaplain and he will pass it on.

About & contact

A student society, run by students, open to everyone.

ISOC DMU has one job: make sure nobody at De Montfort feels like they are doing university on their own. Muslim or not, practising or nowhere near it, you are welcome at almost everything we run. Bring your questions.

Everyone is welcome

You do not need to be a member, know anybody, dress a certain way or know a single word of Arabic to walk into one of our events. Most people arrive on their own the first time.

If you are not Muslim and you are curious, come to a discussion night or just ask through the Q&A. Nobody will try to convert you and nobody will be annoyed by the question.
