Membership covers a slice of what we do. Donations cover the rest: the food at every circle, the Ramadan iftars that feed hundreds, and the mats, Qurans and supplies in the prayer room.

Where your money goes

Every penny is held in the society account at the Students' Union and the accounts are read out at the AGM.

Sadaqah, zakat or a one-off

Tell us in the reference if your gift is zakat and we'll keep it separate, since zakat can only be spent on those eligible to receive it.

Give something other than money

Dates, water, prayer mats and Qurans are always needed, especially in Ramadan. Drop them at the prayer room or message us first.
